# Synthetic media for the refinement

> Curated publication, 17 October 2026. The owner approved the HMS/legal audit as the requirements-analysis baseline and retained refined Concept C. Historical review/approval status below records the earlier authoring stage. [Current approval, product boundaries and publication scope](../../../README.md) govern this package. Phase 1B.3b is not authorized by this publication. Generated QA records and raw research downloads remain local.

The large fixture uses **23 distinct associated image assets**: 20 newly generated photographs and three images reused from the original concept set. The viewer resolves them to the matching JPEG derivatives under `assets/print/`. No old asset was edited or overwritten. The images are fictional AI-generated design material, not inspection evidence.

Generation used the **built-in `image_gen` tool**, with no API key or CLI fallback. [Exact prompts](prompts.json) record the new assets. Original source PNGs remain preserved locally. This publication includes the 23 JPEG derivatives actually used by the approved refined viewer, rather than unused duplicate PNGs. The three reused source prompts are documented in the [preserved asset provenance](../../phase-1b3a/assets/PROVENANCE.md).

The images were generated independently. They do not prove a consistent real property, room dimensions, capture session, measurement, person, location, date or chronology. Some furnishings/materials vary between views; these are **layout/evidence-handling test images**, not a survey of a real apartment. Visible content was reviewed against captions and associated observations; no defect extent, dimension or cause is inferred from generation prompts.

Every figure is explicitly labelled **Tölvugerð sýnimynd** and **Ekki frá raunverulegri skoðun**. The long-text stress fixture deliberately makes M018 unavailable. Its reference and source-index entry remain; the viewer does not silently substitute another photograph.

Images fit within their frames without cropping. The final refined renderer embeds the JPEG derivatives and uses internal report navigation; it emits no image-file opening links. Native diagnostic A4 printing embeds the images for visual QA; no production image storage or report export feature exists.
