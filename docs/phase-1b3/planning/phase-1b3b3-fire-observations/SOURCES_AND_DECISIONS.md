## Heating recommendation

The audit's G20 is a real gap: `model.ts` includes lighting, doors and supplied kitchen/sanitary fixtures, but no dedicated heating/visible-control item. Kitchen `oven` / **Ofn** is a cooking appliance and cannot stand in for space heating. Synthetic Concept C heating records do not establish production support.

**Recommend a separate follow-up after this fire slice.** Fire observations need presence/examination/test semantics distinct from ordinary condition fields. Heating needs a decision about relevant rooms, supplied heating/control types and limitations, and therefore template behavior and new-report versus existing-draft treatment. Joining them here would make the smallest fire contract harder to approve and verify.

If separately authorized, propose a modest **Hitun og sýnileg stjórntæki** observation for relevant inspected rooms: what heating/control was visible; visible condition; whether ordinary user operation was actually tried; observed response or untested/unknown; location, notes and access limitations. Decide applicability explicitly. Do not add boiler service, pressure/temperature measurements, electrical safety, combustion/chimney checks, maintenance validity or professional heating certification. Do not backfill old rooms or repurpose cooking-appliance records.

Deferral leaves the heating gap visible in the phase boundary and future report-readiness/Concept C information mapping. Even with every fire prompt answered, do not describe the whole rental-inspection package as complete. This task does not add a heating-missing penalty that older drafts cannot address.
