## Owner product decisions

| ID | Decision requested | Recommended position / effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| O1 | What does Phase 1B.3b promise? | An attributable, context-aware **draft record and preview**, not a finalized statutory declaration or professional safety assessment. |
| O2 | Approve the four common capture groups? | Context, actual inspection date, recorder name and scope/limitations; never backfill unknown old drafts. Reuse existing identity/answer fields. |
| O3 | Approve minimal rental-only information? | Property number/extent where relevant, landlord/tenant names, lease date/reference. Keep national IDs/contacts out of universal capture; agree their conditional handling with counsel. |
| O4 | Approve the residential fire-observation contract? | Three modest observation groups: detectors, extinguishers/service-label facts, visible escape routes; explicit missing/untested status. No automatic safety pass or hard-coded area-only detector sufficiency. |
| O5 | What stays out of the first implementation slice? | Authenticated participation, signatures, frozen versions, permanent media, actual legal notifications, lease registration, automatic comparisons, payment and other asset types. |
| O6 | Scope optional handover modules | Meter data is conditional at relevant rental handover; keys/inventory are contextual. Allow deferral only with clear missing-data limits and no claim of a completed lease package. |
| O7 | Narrow no-response wording | Approve the app-state principle while acknowledging statutory notice/deeming rules; lawyer approves rental wording. Do not convert silence into an app approval event. |
| O8 | Whether to target examiner-led statutory use | Defer that promise until appointment, authority, attendance, signing and distribution are legally reviewed. A party's own condition record can still be useful without being labeled that formal route. |
| O9 | Historical leases and material technical issues | No automatic historical-law or expert-qualification engine in 1B.3b; identify uncertainty and refer for appropriate advice. |

## Questions for an Icelandic lawyer

| ID | Exact question | Proposed boundary until confirmed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L-Q1 | Precisely when do Arts. 71-72's attendance, detailed declaration, three copies and examiner signature requirements apply to an ordinary party-led inspection under Art. 69(1), where no independent examiner is requested? | State the examiner-led route explicitly; do not say a two-party digital draft completes the formal declaration requirement. |
| L-Q2 | What is the defensible process if a party refuses, fails to attend, disputes the proposed examiner or withholds signature? Can any substitute procedure be described, and what evidence must be kept? | Record actual facts and missing/contested participation. No implied attendance, approval, waiver or unilateral appointment. |
| L-Q3 | Which electronic signature and identical-copy distribution processes satisfy the applicable Arts. 13/72 requirements? What signer identity, authority, version and receipt evidence is necessary? | Typed names, image signatures, OTP access and viewed status are not represented as legal execution. Paper execution may be possible outside the app; do not imply the app implements it. |
| L-Q4 | Is a referenced/attached condition PDF sufficient for the Art. 69 lease inspection section, or must principal results be reproduced there? How should Art. 8 signing of lease additions be handled? | Offer draft principal results, without a claim of completed incorporation or automatic amendment. |
| L-Q5 | Which party/property identifiers must appear on the inspection declaration itself, as distinct from the associated lease? Can national IDs remain in the lease without duplicating them in every report? | Minimize personal data; no universal national-ID requirement for standalone records. |
| L-Q6 | Confirm the appropriate wording distinguishing app no-response state from Art. 16's condition-objection consequences and Arts. 13/64's notice requirements. | Narrow app copy; no statement that silence can never have legal consequences. No automatic notice/deadline service. |
| L-Q7 | For leases predating 2023/2024 and later renewals/extensions, which inspection/signature provisions apply, including party-agreed application of later law? | Record dates/uncertainty; no automatic applicability verdict. J1 confirms that original lease date can matter. |
| L-Q8 | What expertise is appropriate for a requested examiner or a specialized moisture/fire/electrical assessment in a particular case? Are additional sector rules applicable? | No universal examiner-license rule inferred from Art. 69, and no claim that lay app observations replace a suitably qualified specialist. |
| L-Q9 | Approve the privacy/legal basis, access, retention and evidential policy before real party identity data and durable household photographs are processed in a shared service. | Existing local prototype remains a prototype; secure personal data and evidence are separate future infrastructure. |

These questions do not prevent approval of a truthful draft-preview information contract. They do prevent representing unresolved formal execution, technical adequacy or legal-compliance claims as settled product capabilities.
