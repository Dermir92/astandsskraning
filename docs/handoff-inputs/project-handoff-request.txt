### B. PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md

Record all established product decisions.

Important decisions include:

- One shared platform with asset-specific inspection templates.
- Residential inspections use Icelandic HMS inspection practices as a reference, without claiming automatic legal compliance.
- Guided inspections and condition checklists.
- Condition, cleanliness and functionality recorded separately when appropriate.
- Initial and final condition reports.
- Photos attached to specific rooms, items and observations.
- Secure counterparty invitations without mandatory account registration.
- An invitation link alone must not grant report access.
- One-time email verification codes and appropriately scoped access.
- Counterparties can comment, add photos and dispute individual findings.
- Parties cannot silently alter each other's submitted evidence.
- Finalized reports must preserve history and support traceable amendments.
- Viewing a report is not the same as accepting liability.
- No response must never be interpreted as acceptance.
- The platform does not automatically determine responsibility for damage.
- Professional PDF comparison reports are planned.
- Mobile-first inspection workflows.
- Accessibility is a core requirement.

Clearly identify which decisions are approved product direction but not yet implemented.

Pricing hypotheses:

- Initial inspections and counterparty participation are free.
- Proposed individual final report price: ISK 1,990.
- Business subscription pricing remains provisional.
- A promotional offer involving approximately 70 free reports or credits is under consideration.
- Exact promotional eligibility, allocation and duration are NOT finalized.
- No real payment processing is authorized at this stage.

Do not turn unresolved pricing questions into final decisions.

### C. UX_UI_HANDOFF.md

Document our design journey and approved direction.

The original dashboard was rejected because it resembled a generic AI-generated SaaS interface.

Problems included:

- Oversized dark sidebar.
- Excessive cards and containers.
- Large highlighted progress panel.
- Excessive whitespace.
- Generic dashboard composition.
- Weak product identity.
- Insufficient readability of some secondary information.

We explored two color directions and selected the navy/petrol/teal direction (Option A) as the preferred starting point.

A prominent brown/gold accent is not desired.

We subsequently created two fundamentally different layout concepts.

**Concept 1**
- Compact top navigation.
- Reports-first layout.
- Simple data table.
- Straightforward mobile experience.

**Concept 2**
- Compact navigation rail.
- Reports list and contextual details panel.
- Stronger inspection-specific product identity.

**Approved direction for the next implementation phase:**

- Concept 2 as the desktop foundation.
- Simpler mobile experience inspired by Concept 1.
- On mobile, show contextual details through an accessible drawer or bottom sheet where appropriate.
- Refine the contextual panel to emphasize essential information.
- Maintain high text readability.
- Use a professional Nordic B2B design language.
- Avoid generic dashboard cards and unnecessary visual decoration.
- Preserve existing application functionality.

IMPORTANT: This direction has been selected for implementation, but do not claim it is already implemented if the repository still contains the old production UI.

Only the shared application shell and "Mínar skýrslur" screen have been approved for the immediate redesign. Other screens are not yet authorized for redesign.

Accessibility:

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Normal text contrast at least 4.5:1.
- Relevant non-text components at least 3:1.
- Strong visible focus indicators.
- Keyboard accessibility.
- Excellent readability on desktop and mobile.

Previous design comparison results reportedly included:
- Chromium visual review at 1440, 390 and 360 px.
- 17 interface states passing axe checks.
- Minimum reported text contrast 6.38:1.
- Minimum reported control contrast 3.85:1.

Verify these against available artifacts and reports before recording them as confirmed results.
